## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Earlier performance characterizations revealed an apparent paradox:
- **Prior Benchmark Baseline (under normal run-spike.ps1)**: **~12.46 FPS**
- **Transport A/B Clean Cold Boot (under pipe)**: **56.75 ~ 59.93 FPS**

Investigation identified that `run-spike.ps1` historically injected:
`setprop debug.sf.latch_unsignaled 1`
`setprop debug.sf.disable_backpressure 1`
and allowed resuming from Quick Boot snapshots without `-no-snapshot`.

To isolate the root cause, we performed a **systematic 4-way cold-boot A/B experiment** under fixed hardware acceleration (`hw.gltransport=pipe`, `hw.gpu.mode=host`, 1920×1200 @ 280dpi, Telemetry OFF, 5 trials × 30s) and validated the normal product launch path.

## 3. [INFERENCE] Root Cause Analysis & Empirical Discovery

1. **Clean Cold Boot delivers Solid 60 FPS natively**:
   - In a clean cold boot environment with `hw.gltransport=pipe`, Android SurfaceFlinger compositor presentation reaches **59.90 ~ 59.97 FPS** natively without any property tuning.
2. **SurfaceFlinger Properties provide No Throughput Benefit**:
   - Neither `latch_unsignaled=1` nor `disable_backpressure=1` provides any throughput improvement (+0.07 FPS delta is within statistical noise). In fact, `latch_unsignaled=1` increased P50 frame latency from **22.83 ms to 41.76 ms**.
3. **Root Cause of Historical 12.46 FPS Bottleneck**:
   - The historical ~12 FPS limitation was caused by **stale Quick Boot snapshot resumption** and lack of `-no-snapshot` isolation in the initial launch scripts, where GPU/IPC state degraded over successive runs.
   - When launched clean with `-no-snapshot`, the platform consistently operates at **59.94 FPS**.
